## About

Jungle Trail is a first-person walk down a winding jungle trail into overgrown
stone ruins with a waterfall, built in Three.js with **zero external art
assets**. Every texture, mesh, and sound in the scene is generated
procedurally in code — no image files, no models, no audio recordings, no
material libraries.
