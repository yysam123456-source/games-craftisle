Retro ROM Player

Play your own retro game backups right in the browser. No install, no server, 100% client-side.

Click to choose or drop a ROM here Supported: .nes .smc .gba .gb .gbc .md .gg .sms .a26 .iso .bin .n64 …
Important: This player does not include any games. You must supply your own ROM files — typically backups of cartridges you legally own. We do not host, link to, or distribute copyrighted ROMs. All emulation runs locally in your browser via the open-source EmulatorJS engine; your file never leaves your device.
