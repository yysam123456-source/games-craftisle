How to Play

  • 🚶 W A S D or the arrow keys walk · hold Shift to run
  • 👆 Tap the ground → your explorer walks over (goes around the river and over the little bridge)
  • 🖱️ Drag to orbit the camera · scroll or pinch to zoom
  • 🦁 Tap an animal or object → hear its name and its call, and pick up the Chinese word too · collect all 10 animal friends
  • 🤖 Tap the robot guide, or the 🤖 button → it walks the whole route for you
  • 🗺️ Press M or the 🗺️ button → look down over the whole clay world
  • 🏷️ Press L or the 🏷️ button → hide / show the floating bilingual labels (they still pop up on hover)
