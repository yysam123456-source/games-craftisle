# Psiral

A spiral-pattern arcade game and winner of the 2013 GitHub Game Off jam — climb the spiral as fast as you can.

## How to Play

Trace the spiral outward without hitting the wall. The further you get, the faster it turns.

## Controls

- **Mouse — steer the cursor, Click — change direction**
