# The House

You are inside a strange house. Find the way out — if there is one. A short, unsettling browser puzzle about rooms that do not quite add up.

## How to Play

Explore each room, interact with what you find, and work out how the house fits together. It is short. It is not kind.

## Controls

- **WASD / arrows — move, Mouse — look, Click — interact with objects**
