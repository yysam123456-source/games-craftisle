# I Spy A Ghost

An experimental peer-to-peer multiplayer hide-and-seek game built with HTML5 and Phaser, using WebRTC to connect two players directly.

## How to Play

One player hides, one seeks, and the browser connects you straight to the other player over WebRTC — no server in between.

## Controls

- **WASD — move, Mouse — look, Click — interact**
