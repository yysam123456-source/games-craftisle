# Sandboxels

A falling-sand physics sandbox with over 500 materials. Paint elements, ignite fire, drip water and watch them react — every material is simulated with real granular physics.

## How to Play

Pick a material from the sidebar, then draw it into the world. Combine elements to discover new ones — every one of the 500+ materials reacts with fire, water and each other in real time.

## Controls

- **Left click — paint element, Right click — erase, Scroll — brush size, Number keys — pick material, Space — pause**
