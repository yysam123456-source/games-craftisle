# Duck Hunt JS

The NES Duck Hunt ported to JavaScript and HTML5 — complete with a level editor so you can build your own hunting stages.

## How to Play

Shoot the ducks before they fly off the screen, and mind the grass — the hunting dog is not your enemy, but he is in the way. Includes a level editor for building your own stages.

## Controls

- **Mouse — aim, Click — shoot, 1-2 — switch weapon, Esc — back to menu**
