# Push and Fork

A puzzle game where you hold a fork, shove blocks, and rewind time to solve the level. Made for Global Game Jam 2012.

## How to Play

Push the blocks into place, and when you get stuck, rewind and try a different line. Made in a weekend for a game jam.

## Controls

- **Arrow keys / WASD — move and push, Space — rewind time**
