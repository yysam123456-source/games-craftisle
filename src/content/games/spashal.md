# Spashal

Spashal is a retro vertical shooter by **MrRar**, presented with a deliberately period-accurate look - a scrolling marquee ticker, a logo banner and keyboard shortcuts advertised in plain text across the top.

Gameplay is a straightforward vertical shooter: fly up the screen, clear the waves, and watch your score climb. What is unusual is the shell around it - the game ships with working pause, menu, screenshot and full-screen buttons, plus a debug HUD, all keyboard-driven with **P**, **M**, **Q** and **F**. It is a fork of KrofDrakula's *Squirts* with the same source, so it is best played for its presentation rather than for anything the original did not do.

## How to Play

1. Fly up the screen and shoot the incoming enemies.
2. Use the on-screen buttons or the advertised shortcuts for pause, menu, screenshot and full screen.
3. Survive the waves and beat your score.

## Controls

- **Arrow keys** - move
- **Space** - fire
- **P** - pause
- **M** - menu
- **Q** - screenshot
- **F** - full screen

## Tips

Use the screenshot key rather than a browser extension. It captures the canvas at its native resolution, so the marquee and HUD are included and the result is not cropped by the page viewport.
