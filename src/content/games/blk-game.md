# BLK

A fully editable multiplayer voxel world running efficiently in the browser, with block placing and breaking.

## How to Play

Dig into the world and build back out of it. Everything is editable, and the world is shared.

## Controls

- **WASD — move, Space — jump, Mouse — look, Click — place block, Right-click — break**

## Open Source & License

BLK is created by **morozd** and distributed under the **GPL-2.0**.
