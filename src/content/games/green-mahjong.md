# Green Mahjong

A single-player mahjong solitaire built in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Clear every tile, take your time.

## How to Play

Match free tiles by type and clear the whole board. No timer, no pressure — a quiet afternoon of solitaire.

## Controls

- **Click — pick up and place a tile**
