# The Pond

A relaxing exploration game about a quiet pond. Nothing chases you, nothing scores you — just wander, watch the water, and find your way back.

## How to Play

There is no goal here. Drift across the water, watch the light change, and take your time.

## Controls

- **WASD / arrows — swim, Mouse — look around**
