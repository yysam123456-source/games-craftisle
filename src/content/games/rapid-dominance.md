# Rapid Dominance

Rapid Dominance is a real-time strategy game by **Wenta**, built with **Phaser 3** and TypeScript. Players take control of a grid of fields, and each captured field produces income that funds further expansion - so every turn is a choice between securing what you have and reaching for what you do not.

The seven maps scale from a two-player duel up to sixteen, and the twelve game modes cover everything from free-for-all deathmatch to structured team games. A round takes a couple of minutes, which makes it well suited to a quick break. Ships with an in-game About and Instruction screen, so it needs no external documentation.

## How to Play

1. Choose a map and a game mode from the welcome screen.
2. Capture adjacent fields to expand your territory.
3. Collect gold each round from the fields you hold.
4. Out-expand or outlast your opponents to win.

## Controls

- **Click** - select a field or menu item
- **Enter** - confirm
- **Esc** - back

## Tips

Income scales with the fields you hold, not the ones you capture. A compact cluster of connected fields is worth far more than the same number scattered across the map, because scattered fields cost the same to hold but cannot be defended.
