# Particle Clicker

An addictive incremental clicker that teaches you the history of high-energy particle physics while you click. Every particle you buy is a real discovery.

## How to Play

Click to gather energy, then buy the accelerators that generate more. Each unlock is a genuine particle from the history of physics — the first hadron collider in a browser tab.

## Controls

- **Click — collect energy, 1-6 — buy particle accelerators, Shift — quick buy**
