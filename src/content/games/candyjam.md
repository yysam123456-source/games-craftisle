# Candy Jam

A candy-themed HTML5 shooter and tower-defense hybrid, made for the Candy Jam game jam.

## How to Play

Defend the candy base from waves of hungry sweets. Spend the points you earn on more towers before the next wave lands.

## Controls

- **Mouse — aim and click, 1-5 — switch tower type, Space — start wave**
