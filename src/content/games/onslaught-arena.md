# Onslaught Arena

A fast-paced arcade shooter where you hold off waves of classic medieval monsters. A development build of the full game.

## How to Play

Survive the waves. The monsters come in numbers, and they do not stop coming.

## Controls

- **WASD — move, Mouse — aim, Click — attack, Space — special**

## Open Source & License

Onslaught Arena is created by **lostdecade**.

- Source repository: <https://github.com/lostdecade/onslaught_arena>
