# Avabranch

A minimalist endless jumper. Guide Ava through an endless corridor of moving blocks and keep moving as long as you can.

## How to Play

One button, one endless corridor. Time the jumps and keep the run alive for as long as possible.

## Controls

- **Click / Space — jump, Mouse — steer**
