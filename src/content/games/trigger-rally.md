# Trigger Rally

A fast-paced arcade rally racer built with Three.js and WebGL. Drift around hairpin corners and blast through checkpoints in a rally car that wants to leave the road.

## How to Play

Pick a track, then push the throttle. The faster you enter a corner the more the car slides — learn the racing line and chain drifts for maximum speed.

## Controls

- **Arrow keys — steer and accelerate, Space — handbrake drift, R — reset**
