# Iso Middle Earth - Isometric Realm Builder

## Game Overview

Iso Middle Earth is an **isometric realm-building sandbox** set in the world of Tolkien's Middle-earth. No internet, no sign-up — just open and play. Pick a realm (the Shire, Gondor, Mordor, Rohan, Lothlórien, Moria, Rivendell, or a Mixed landscape), freely paint terrain tiles across a grid, and place hobbits, trees, buildings, and decorations to assemble your own Middle-earth domain like a box of LEGO.

All progress is auto-saved in your browser (localStorage), so it won't be lost when you close the tab. Come back anytime to keep creating, or export your work as an image or a JSON file to share with friends.

## Features

- **Multiple realm themes** — the peaceful countryside of the Shire, the scorched wastes of Mordor, the elven sanctuary of Rivendell… each realm ships its own set of terrain tiles.
- **Isometric art** — classic 2.5D isometric view gives your map instant depth and Middle-earth atmosphere.
- **Free creation** — adjustable grid size (3×3 up to 20×20); build as big as you like.
- **Rich assets** — hobbits, trees, all kinds of buildings and decorations, placed exactly where you want.
- **One-click export** — render your creation as a PNG image, or export a JSON project file to keep editing later.
- **Pure client-side** — zero backend, zero database. Runs smoothly locally; your data lives only in your own browser.

## How to Play

### 1. Choose a Realm
After entering the game, pick a Middle-earth realm from the **location dropdown** in the top toolbar — e.g. "Shire", "Mordor", "Rivendell", or "Mixed". The realm you choose determines the terrain style available to you.

### 2. Paint Terrain
- In the **Asset Picker**, select a terrain tile (grass, road, water, rock, etc.).
- **Click** a cell on the canvas grid, or **click-and-drag** to paint that tile continuously — fill the landscape like drawing.
- **Right-click** any cell to clear it back to empty land, making re-planning easy.

### 3. Place Characters & Buildings
- Switch the Asset Picker to the characters/buildings category and choose **hobbits**, trees, houses, and more.
- Click a target cell on the grid to place the item; right-click also clears it.
- Combine terrain + buildings + characters to gradually assemble a village, a fortress, or an entire kingdom.

### 4. Adjust & Undo
- The **grid size** button in the top toolbar resizes the map (note: changing size resets the current map).
- The **Undo** button steps back through your last actions (shortcut `⌘/Ctrl + Z`).
- The **Clear** button resets everything to a blank map so you can start over.

### 5. Save & Export
- **Export PNG** — render your current creation into an image downloaded to your device.
- **Export / Import JSON** — save the whole map as a JSON project file; import it next time to keep editing.
- The game **auto-saves** to your browser, so reopening the page restores your last creation automatically.

## Controls

- **Mouse / Touch** — click or drag on the grid to paint tiles; right-click to clear a single cell.
- **Switch assets** — use the bottom or side Asset Picker to select different tiles, characters, and building categories.
- **Switch realm** — use the top dropdown to change the Middle-earth realm theme.
- **Keyboard shortcut** — `⌘ / Ctrl + Z` to undo the last step.
- **Grid size** — top toolbar button adjusts the map's width/height (3–20).

## Tips

- Lay down "grass + roads" to form a main street first, then place houses and hobbits along it — your work will feel more layered.
- Want a riverside town? Switch to a realm with water and use river/lake tiles to frame a harbor.
- Before exporting a PNG, hit "Clear" and try a few versions to pick your favorite to share.
- Your work is saved **locally** — switching browsers or clearing cache will lose it, so export a JSON backup for anything important.
