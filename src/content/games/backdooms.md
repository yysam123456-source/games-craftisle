# The Backdooms

A backrooms-themed first-person shooter inspired by 1993's DOOM. Explore liminal office corridors, find scrap, and survive whatever is in the dark.

## How to Play

Collect scrap to survive. The corridors shift and the things in them do not want you to leave.

## Controls

- **WASD — move, Mouse — aim, Click — fire, Shift — sprint, E — interact**
